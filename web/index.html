THE ANSWER / SYNTHESIS CONCLUSIONS

What did bringing them together reveal?

Three findings from the original analyses and one joint interpretation: describing movement, anticipating a label and quantifying consequences require different evidence.

01 / MOVEMENT

Movement needs validated measurement.

RIC contributes a 30-person technical pilot; its measurements do not establish an injury biomarker.

02 / TRAINING

It beats chance only marginally.

Unseen athletes, future period: AP 0.020 against 0.016 for chance; the paired CI of AP minus chance (0.0020 to 0.0270) excludes zero. With an AUC of 0.546 it is not clinically useful.

03 / CONSEQUENCES

Frequency and burden change the ranking.

Hamstring injury leads record counts; Cruciate ligament tear leads summed episode-days.

JOINT CONCLUSION

Movement, the probability of a label and the impact of an absence tell different stories.

The integration turns three separate analyses into a common research framework: measure current function with quality, evaluate training signals in unseen people and future periods, and describe impact using frequency and consequence measures. The result is a complementary interpretation, with each conclusion linked to its source.

What remains unresolved: how a movement characteristic relates to load and absence duration in the same person. These sources study different populations; that association still needs a linked cohort.

JOINT RESULT / WHAT CHANGED WHEN COMBINED

From separate results to connected questions.

01 / RIC

What am I measuring now?

Biomechanics contributes a movement measurement. Its meaning depends on speed, protocol, side and event quality.

02 / RUNNERS

What does preceding history add?

Load histories add temporal context. Ranking future labels must be evaluated out of training with sample selection kept visible.

03 / FOOTBALL

What consequences were recorded?

Frequency and duration provide distinct views of impact. A label can be less frequent yet accumulate many days of absence.

What this project produced

A research framework separating measurement, anticipation and impact; a catalogue of 104 definitions; three documented outcomes; and a map of missing links. No correlations across people from different sources or combined clinical effect were calculated. The gain is a traceable synthesis and a more precise design for the next multimodal study.
