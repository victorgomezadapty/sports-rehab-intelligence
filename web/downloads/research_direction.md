# Research direction and delivery line

**Sports Rehab Intelligence — Movement, training and injury consequences**

## Release 1.0.0

An evidence-compatibility study and public atlas integrating three existing projects. Its original outputs are a common definition catalogue, distinct-outcome registry, authored domain matrix, pinned-source release and linked-cohort specification. The website, Kaggle dataset/notebook, repository and portfolio are publication surfaces for this same version.

## Main question

Which movement, training and injury-consequence constructs are observed, and what identity, measurement, timing, exposure and outcome validation would permit a linked multimodal study?

RIC asks how a runner moves under a defined protocol. Competitive Runners examines histories before selected injury labels. Football describes recorded absence consequences. They address complementary parts of Sports Rehabilitation & Human Performance, with different people, protocols and endpoints. The integrator exposes those relationships while preserving separate cohorts.

## Next analytical work

1. Review each catalogue definition against its measurement protocol and retain ambiguous entries explicitly.
2. Extend the construct map only after documenting a new source's definition, unit, observation grain and endpoint. Keep companion cohorts separately namespaced.
3. Complete RIC's official event/cycle validation before expanding biomechanics inference.
4. Design or acquire a genuinely multimodal cohort. Prespecify the clinical question, missingness rules, available-at times, eligible exposure and validation design.
5. Then evaluate individual associations or a combined model, including uncertainty, transportability and operational usefulness under the new study design.
