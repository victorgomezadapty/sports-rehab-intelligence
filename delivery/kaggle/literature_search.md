# Focused literature verification — 1 November 2026

Purpose: verify primary sources needed to explain source compatibility, outcomes and burden for this three-study atlas. This was a targeted narrative exercise, not a registered or exhaustive systematic review. No PRISMA counts or formal risk-of-bias assessment are claimed.

Starting materials: the three local README files, literature discussions, data dictionaries and handoff limitations. Followed their dataset DOIs and original papers; checked primary publisher, PubMed/PMC and institutional records for exact titles, authors and scope. Search concepts included the exact RIC descriptor title, Lövdal competitive-runner injury prediction and erratum, IOC 2020 injury/illness surveillance and injury burden.

Verified sources are listed in `config/references.json`. Primary records include:

- RIC descriptor: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11564798/ — verified exact title, study scope and data citation to Figshare v1. A newer indexed version was not substituted for the source version used locally.
- Competitive Runners original article: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33931574/ — verified authors, title and population. Dataset DOI and authors were also checked through the University of Groningen institutional record because Dataverse presented an automated-access barrier: https://research.rug.nl/nl/datasets/replication-data-for-injury-prediction-in-competitive-runners-wit/ . The exact corrected lag suffix order remains grounded in the pinned source project and its corrected source README, not inferred from a bibliographic abstract.
- IOC consensus: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7146946/ — definitions, exposure and reporting distinctions.
- Burden editorial: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29021247/ — frequency and consequences as distinct dimensions.
